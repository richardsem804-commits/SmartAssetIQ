How SmartAssetIQ's AI actually works

From fleet data to funded decisions, explained.

Every asset is scored the instant its data changes — no waiting on a model, no black box. This page walks through the scoring engine on the current demo fleet, and lets you ask the advisor a question directly.

Engine
How the engine decides

Methodology
What the model weighs
Priority score blends four inputs, each auditable back to the asset register — nothing here is a guess.

Condition & urgency

Blends repair spend as a share of replacement cost (60%) with how much of the asset's useful life has been consumed (40%).

Financial case

Net present value, internal rate of return and payback period of replacing now vs. running the current asset, at your discount rate.

Data confidence

Records with a purchase date, useful life, operating cost and salvage value score "High" confidence; thinner records are flagged and weighted down.

Budget discipline

Every "Replace" recommendation is checked against your annual procurement budget, so priority never ignores affordability.
